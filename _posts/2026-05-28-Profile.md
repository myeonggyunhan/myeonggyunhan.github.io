# Short Bio
  
Myeonggyun Han is an assistant professor in the School of Computer Science and
Engineering at [Kyungpook National University
(KNU)](https://en.knu.ac.kr/main/main.htm), where he leads the [Computer Systems
and AI Lab (CoSAIL)](https://cosail.knu.ac.kr). His research interests lie in
computer systems and system software for AI, with a focus on efficient large
language model (LLM) inference, on-device and edge AI systems, and system
software support for emerging AI workloads. He received B.S. and Ph.D. degrees
in Computer Science and Engineering from Ulsan National Institute of Science and
Technology (UNIST), under the supervision of [Prof. Woongki
Baek](https://sites.google.com/site/woongkibaek). Prior to joining KNU, he
worked as a researcher at the Electronics and Telecommunications Research
Institute (ETRI).
  
# Research Interests  
- Computer Systems and System Software for AI    
- Efficient LLM Inference and Runtime Optimization    
- Resource Management for On-Device and Edge AI Systems
